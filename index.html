01 — Research

What we work on

Object pose estimation

Keypoint-based pose estimation for extensive object classes, built on the structure-prototype view of deformable categories.

Large-scale datasets

Dataset construction and annotation methodology — see our latest release, PoseImageNet.

Structured representations

Prototypes, skeletons and deformable models as intermediate structure for recognition and generation.
